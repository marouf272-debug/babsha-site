# Surface brief: Babsha website (index)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor decides whether to play and presses Play.
Audience: people with a minute and a group to play with, mostly on phones. Proof: the live game, real tile and card text. No invented numbers, prices or testimonials. Constraints in PRODUCT.md.
Memorable moment: picking a ride lifts the box lid and the pawn you chose drives the board.
Unresolved: the "coming soon" feature name.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is one collector's box seen from above: green felt table, cream lid, brass ride pieces. It refuses the dark glass-panel game landing and the neon night city.

OWN-WORLD: Bottle-green felt (#0A4430 to #052A1D, with grain), cream card stock (#F1E6CC), antique gold foil (#D4B35E) for hairlines and double rules, red only on the red die and the rickshaw. Display serif (Gloock) over a clean grotesk. Panels are cream cards with a gold double border and chapter numerals. The 3D board is green lacquer with gold inlay and cream ceramic tiles. No glass blur, no rainbow colours, no neon.

STORY: A visitor sees a game worth paying for, picks a ride, watches it drive the board one stop at a time, learns the rules in a line each, and presses Play.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Felt table. A cream box lid, 1100 by 520 at 1440 wide, centred high: the title Roll. Build. Win. at left, the tilted board art at right. Below it three gold-rimmed brass medallions (rickshaw, CNG, bus) and two dice resting on the felt. Wordmark top centre, Play now top right. Picking a medallion lifts the lid away and reveals the 3D board.

FORM: Collector's box (position 1 on my ordered list, IMPECCABLE'S PICK), seed:1a18ea040a4daa67.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
