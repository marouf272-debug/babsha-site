Everyone starts rich.

Everyone starts with the same cash, and passing GO pays you every lap. Two to six players on one phone, or take on the computer.

Streets for sale.

Land on a street nobody owns and you can buy it. Pass, and it goes to a live auction.

Build it up.

Own a whole color set and its rent doubles. Then build, house by house, up to four, with a hotel on top.

Stuck in traffic.

Roll three doubles in a row, land on Go to Jam, or draw the wrong card and you are in Jam. Pay the fine, roll doubles or play a card to get out.

Run the routes.

Kamalapur, Sadarghat, Gabtoli and the airport. The more hubs you own, the more every visit costs. DESCO and WASA charge by the dice: 40 times your roll, or 100 if you own both.

Pull up a chair.

Cha Adda is a free rest stop: no rent, no fine. It is also where deals get made. Trade properties and cash with any player, mortgages and all.

Roll big.

Doubles give you another roll, and a double six carries you twelve tiles in one throw. The green set is pricey, so the rent hurts.

Pull a card.

Metro Rail rides, flooded roads, kite contests and load-shedding. Land on Lucky or DOHS and Dhaka happens to you.

Finish rich.

The last stretch of the board is the most valuable. Run out of money and you are bankrupt. The last player standing wins.

Everything a board game night needs.

No download, no account. Open it and play.

  1. Pass and play

    Two to six players on one phone. Hand it over, tap to start, roll.

  2. Play the computer

    Easy, Medium or Hard opponents that buy, bid, build and trade.

  3. Live auctions

    Pass on a purchase and it goes to the whole table.

  4. Trade

    Swap properties and cash to finish a set first.

  5. Build and mortgage

    Houses go up evenly across a set, with a hotel on top. Mortgage a property when cash runs short.

  6. Install it

    Add it to your home screen. It works offline.

  7. House rules

    Choose your starting cash and add an optional turn timer.

  8. No account

    Open the link and play. Nothing to download or sign up for.
