Babsha logo options

Pick one (or mix). Wordmark is Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold. Final file will be outlined SVG.

1. Rickshaw Wheel

The painted wheel of a Dhaka rickshaw: red rim, yellow ring, teal spokes, pink hub. Reads as Dhaka at a glance, friendly, works as a round app icon.

2. Double Six

Two dice, both sixes, the best roll in the game. Most direct "this is a board game" signal and it matches the dice-roll scroll on the site.

3. Skyline Tile

A board tile in the game's own green and cream, with a Dhaka skyline standing on a red set-color band. Closest to the actual game art, strongest as a favicon and badge.
